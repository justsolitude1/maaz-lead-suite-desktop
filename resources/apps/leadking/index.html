2 · RESEARCH

Check saved websites

◉

Deep mode uses Playwright to render practice sites and prioritizes contact, about, team, and clinician pages so it can capture each named practitioner's bio while crawling. For each named practitioner still missing an individual email, the enabled-by-default automatic fallback makes one separate DuckDuckGo search using only “name + email”—without the practice name, city, or role—and reviews up to five result pages for that person. The per-person Google button opens the same query so you can inspect Google results and its AI Overview manually; the app does not treat the overview as source evidence. Search snippets and AI summaries are discovery clues only. Any address they surface stays unverified unless a linked public source ties the exact name and email to that same person's professional context. Current practice affiliation is shown separately and does not decide whether a matching person's public professional email is retained. The crawler obeys robots.txt and per-domain delays, and stops on 403/429/CAPTCHA blocks without bypassing them. Shared/role inboxes are excluded. Approval still requires the named person, exact email, and same-person evidence. Solo-practice evidence is optional and stored separately.

Legal & responsible use

This tool is designed for public business contact research, not personal-data harvesting.

Live sources in this build

OpenStreetMap via Overpass and Photon geocoding, public business websites, user-supplied domain lists, and Google Maps when enabled. The original app warns that Google Maps scraping is against its Terms of Service; review current terms before use.

Use business contact details responsibly. The crawler observes robots.txt, limits requests, and stops when Google presents a CAPTCHA or access block. Your team is responsible for reviewing source terms and applicable laws. Do not use this workflow to collect private or personal contact lists.
